# Disciplines

The structure of every zeevolution document is the same for every discipline. Only the **words for work** and the **examples** change. Use this table to pick them.

| | Engineering | Data Science | Design | Product Management |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Work artifacts** | pull requests, RFCs / design docs, services, runbooks | analyses, models, experiments, dashboards, notebooks | flows, prototypes, research reports, design-system components | specs / PRDs, discovery briefs, roadmaps, launch plans |
| **Typical evidence** | PRs merged, incidents avoided, latency / reliability numbers, adoption of a library or pattern | decisions influenced, model performance, experiment readouts used by others | usability results, adoption of components, critiques led, research reused | outcomes moved (activation, retention, revenue), decisions unblocked, launches |
| **Review forum** | code review, design review | analysis review, model review | design critique | product review, roadmap review |
| **"Done" means** | live, healthy and adopted | trusted and used to decide | shipped and validated with users | outcome moved, not just feature shipped |
| **Common growth gap** | from tasks to owning a scope; from "code is right" to "it's live and adopted" | from answering questions to shaping which questions matter | from one option to converging on decisions | from managing a backlog to setting direction |

## Example lines by discipline

Use these to swap a template example for the person's discipline.

**Engineering**
- Brag: "Moved the search service to the new cache layer: p95 latency down from 420 ms to 180 ms, no incidents during rollout."
- Rule: "Half a day, not two: when stuck for half a day, bring two options to the team."

**Data Science**
- Brag: "Rebuilt the demand forecast; operations now plans with it every week."
- Rule: "One decision memo a month that a PM acts on."

**Design**
- Brag: "Ran two critique sessions for new hires; both now present their own flows in design review."
- Rule: "Every critique ends with a decision in at most two rounds."

**Product Management**
- Brag: "Wrote the checkout problem brief; reused by two other squads."
- Rule: "Every risk in the weekly update has an owner, a date and a decision."

## Other disciplines

If the user says "Other" (e.g. QA, security, developer relations, engineering management), ask one follow-up — "What does a typical piece of your work look like, and how do you know it went well?" — and use the answer as the artifacts and evidence for that person.
