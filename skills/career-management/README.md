# Career management

Managing a career — your own, or the people you lead.

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [career-hub-toolkit](career-hub-toolkit/SKILL.md) | user-invoked | One command builds the Career Hub and every career document |
| [career-ladder](career-ladder/SKILL.md) | model-invoked | Map a company ladder to levels × pillars, or use the generic one |

The **Career Hub** is the entry point of this category: later career skills read and update the hub the toolkit created.

Bundled copies under `*/assets/` and `*/scripts/` are generated by `scripts/sync_skill_assets.py` from `templates/` and `skills/foundations/cloud-workspace/scripts/`. Don't edit them directly.
