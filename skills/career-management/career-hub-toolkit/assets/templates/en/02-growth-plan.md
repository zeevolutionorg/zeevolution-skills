> 💡 **How to use:** turn each focus into a **move** (a behavior) and a **rule** you can check, with a number in it. A rule without a number can't be checked.
>
> ✍️ **Example (Engineering):** Move: "Unblock yourself faster." Rule: "Half a day, not two: when stuck for half a day, bring two options to the team."
>
> ✍️ **Example (Design):** Move: "Converge faster." Rule: "Every critique ends with a decision in at most two rounds."
>
> ✍️ **Example (Product):** Move: "Make risks visible." Rule: "Every risk in the weekly update has an owner, a date and a decision."
