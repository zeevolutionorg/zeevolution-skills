## 🤝 Part 1 — Working Agreement *(once, in the first 1:1)*

> 💡 **How to use:** these five questions shape how you work together. Ask them in the first 1:1, write the answers in the person's own words, and act on them. Revisit them when something changes (new team, new leader, a rough period).

### 1. How do you like your work to be followed up on?

- 

> ✍️ **Example:** "Async updates in the ticket are enough. Only ping me directly when something is blocked for more than a day."

### 2. How do you like to be recognized?

- 

> ✍️ **Example:** "In private first. Public shout-outs only for team wins, not for me individually."

### 3. How, and how often, do you want feedback?

- 

> ✍️ **Example:** "Straight away and direct, no polishing. Save the bigger patterns for the bi-weekly."

### 4. When in the day do you do your best work?

- 

> ✍️ **Example:** "Mornings for deep work. I'd rather have meetings after lunch."

### 5. Is anything about your routine non-negotiable?

- 

> ✍️ **Example:** "I leave at 6 pm on Tuesdays and Thursdays. If something is critical, we can move the 1:1 earlier."
