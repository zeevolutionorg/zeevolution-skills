## 📏 The scale (always relative to the CURRENT level)

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not yet at level** | Still building what the current level expects. |
| **At level** | Does what the current level expects, reliably. |
| **Strong at level** | Consistently does it well, with little help; a reference for others. |
| **Operating at next level** | Already shows what the next level expects, with evidence. |
| **N/A** | Not applicable this period. |

> 💡 **How to use:** score each pillar twice: once by the person, once by the leader. When the two scores are **two or more steps apart**, mark the row ⚑ and talk about it in the next bi-weekly. "Operating at next level" always needs evidence linked.
