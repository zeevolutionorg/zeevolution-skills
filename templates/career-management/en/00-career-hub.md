## 🔀 Development plan: Growth Plan or Focus Plan

The **Growth Plan** and the **Focus Plan** are two alternative ways to plan development. **Use one, not both.** Both were created so you can choose together, with the real documents in front of you.

| | 🌱 Growth Plan | 🧭 Focus Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Starts from | The gap to the next level: strengths, what's missing, three moves | The work ahead: how time is split, goals, what success looks like |
| Fits best when | Working toward the next level, closing a clear gap, or new to the level | Solid at the level and steering impact, or prefers planning by outcomes |
| Ladder use | Strong: compares current and next level | Light: goals can reference ladder pillars |

**Chosen:** ☐ Growth Plan ☐ Focus Plan · **Decided on:** ________ · **Revisit at:** next Career Check

> 💡 **How to use:** decide in the first bi-weekly or the first Career Check. Then add "Not in use — we chose the other plan on (date)" at the top of the unused one. Keep it: you can switch at a later Career Check.

## 🧭 How this Hub works

- **One entry point.** Start here every time. If a document isn't linked here, it doesn't exist yet.
- **The person owns the career; the leader owns the rhythm.** {{person_name}} writes the reflections, the plan and the Brag. {{leader_name}} makes sure the bi-weekly happens and the feedback is specific.
- **Small and often beats big and rare.** Ten minutes every two weeks keeps everything current; nobody has to rebuild a year of evidence at review time.
- **Evidence over adjectives.** Link the real work: a pull request, an analysis, a design file, a spec, a dashboard.
