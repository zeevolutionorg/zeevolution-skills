# /career-hub-toolkit

Builds a complete career hub for one person, in one command.

```text
/career-hub-toolkit me                                    # self mode: your own career
/career-hub-toolkit Ana Souza, Data Scientist L3, google  # lead mode: someone you lead
```

## What you get

A folder `Career Hub — <name>` with:

| # | Document | Self | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | 🚀 **Career Hub** — the central page; links everything; recommends a bi-weekly 1:1 | ✅ | ✅ |
| 01 | 🎶 1:1 Notes — Working Agreement, bi-weekly 1:1s (SBI), quarterly Career Check, Review Conversation | ✅ | ✅ |
| 02 | 🌱 Growth Plan — gap to next level, three moves with rules | ✅ | ✅ |
| 04 | 🧭 Focus Plan — time split, goals, what success looks like | ✅ | ✅ |
| 03 | 😎 Brag Document — impact log with evidence | ✅ | ✅ |
| 05 | 🪜 Ladder Assessment — automated spreadsheet | ✅ | ✅ |
| 06 | 📝 Performance Review | — | ✅ |
| 07 | 🔎 Evidence Doc — only when a level is at risk | — | optional |
| 08 | 🛬 Onboarding Plan 30/60/90 — only for new hires | — | optional |

**Growth Plan and Focus Plan are alternatives.** Both are created; choose one together in a bi-weekly or Career Check.

## What it asks

Only what it doesn't know yet — usually 5–7 questions after setup. The exact questions, in English and Portuguese, are in [INTERVIEW.md](../../skills/career-management/career-hub-toolkit/INTERVIEW.md). Nothing is created before you confirm.

## The Ladder Assessment spreadsheet

- Pick a self score and a leader score per pillar from a dropdown (Not yet at level · At level · Strong at level · Operating at next level · N/A).
- Expectations for the current and next level fill in automatically when you choose the levels.
- Gaps of two steps or more are flagged "Talk about this".
- The Summary tab shows a chart, averages, and readiness (Building · Close · Ready to discuss promotion) — a prompt for conversation, not a decision.
- Yellow cells are inputs; grey cells are calculated and protected.
- Uses your company's ladder if you share it; otherwise a generic one, clearly marked.

## Privacy

Documents about a real person are created only in your own workspace. Temporary data is deleted after the run. In lead mode, share the folder only with the person.
