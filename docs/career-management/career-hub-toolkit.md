# 🚀 /career-hub-toolkit

> 🌱 **Category: career management** · the entry skill of this category. [All categories →](../README.md)

Builds a complete career hub for one person, in one command.

```text
/career-hub-toolkit me                                    # 👤 self mode: your own career
/career-hub-toolkit Ana Souza, Data Scientist L3, google  # 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 lead mode: someone you lead
```

## 🔄 How it works

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1️⃣ ⚙️ | Loads your saved defaults (from `/setup-zeevolution-skills`) |
| 2️⃣ 💬 | Asks only what's missing — exact questions, EN or pt-BR |
| 3️⃣ 🪜 | Maps your company's ladder. If you don't have one yet, the pillars stay blank to replace after the documents are generated |
| 4️⃣ ✅ | Shows a summary and waits for **"Create"** |
| 5️⃣ 🏗️ | Creates every document, then the **Career Hub last**, with all links |
| 6️⃣ 📣 | Gives you the Hub link, what's still generic, and the first actions |

## 📦 What you get

A folder `Career Hub — <name>` with:

| # | Document | 👤 Self | 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | 🚀 **Career Hub** — the central page; links everything; recommends a bi-weekly 1:1 | ✅ | ✅ |
| 01 | 🎶 1:1 Notes — Working Agreement, bi-weekly 1:1s (SBI), quarterly Career Check, Review Conversation | ✅ | ✅ |
| 02 | 🌱 Growth Plan — gap to next level, three moves with rules | ✅ | ✅ |
| 03 | 😎 Brag Document — impact log with evidence | ✅ | ✅ |
| 04 | 🧭 Focus Plan — time split, goals, what success looks like | ✅ | ✅ |
| 05 | 🪜 Ladder Assessment — automated spreadsheet | ✅ | ✅ |
| 06 | 📝 Performance Review | — | ✅ |
| 07 | 🔎 Evidence Doc — only when a level is at risk | — | optional |
| 08 | 🛬 Onboarding Plan 30/60/90 — only for new hires | — | optional |

> [!NOTE]
> **Growth Plan and Focus Plan are alternatives.** Both are created; you choose one together in a bi-weekly or Career Check. The toolkit never chooses for you.

## 💬 What it asks

Only what it doesn't know yet — usually **5–7 questions** after setup. The exact questions, in English and Portuguese, are in [INTERVIEW.md](../../skills/career-management/career-hub-toolkit/INTERVIEW.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Nothing is created before you confirm.

## 🔌 Where it creates them

| # | Path | You get |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🔗 Google Workspace / Microsoft 365 connector | Documents and a spreadsheet in your workspace, linked from the Hub |
| 2 | 📁 Files | `.docx` + `.xlsx` to open in Word / Excel or upload to Drive |
| 3 | 📋 Manual | Filled-in Markdown + a HOW-TO-PASTE guide — as files or in the chat |

If the connector you asked for isn't there, it says so in one line and moves down the list.

## 🪜 The Ladder Assessment spreadsheet

| Feature | |
|---|---|
| 🔽 **Dropdown scores** | Self and leader score per pillar: Not yet at level · At level · Strong at level · Operating at next level · N/A |
| ⚡ **Auto-filled expectations** | Choose the current and next level; every pillar's expectations fill in |
| ⚑ **Gap flags** | Self and leader two or more steps apart → "Talk about this" |
| 📊 **Summary** | Chart, averages, readiness (Building · Close · Ready to discuss promotion) — a prompt, not a decision |
| 🎨 **Clear design** | Yellow cells are inputs; grey cells are calculated and protected; hover tips on every header |
| 🪜 **Your company's ladder** | Filled in when you share it. Pillar names differ by company and are replaced after the documents are generated |

## 🗓️ After it runs

1. 📅 Book a recurring **bi-weekly 1:1** with the Hub link in the invite (the toolkit offers to create it if your agent has calendar access).
2. 🤝 Fill the **Working Agreement** in the first 1:1.
3. 🧭 Schedule the **Career Check**.
4. 🔀 Choose together between the **Growth Plan** and the **Focus Plan**.

## 🔒 Privacy

- 🏠 Documents about a real person are created **only in your own workspace**.
- 🧹 Temporary data is deleted after the run.
- 🔐 In lead mode, share the folder **only with the person**.
