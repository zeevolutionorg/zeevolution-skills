# 🚀 Start here — Career Hub Manual Kit

> ℹ️ **What this is:** everything you need to build a career hub by hand, with no AI. About 20–30 minutes the first time; about 10 minutes per person with a master copy. Full guide with Google and Microsoft steps: `docs/MANUAL.md` in the repository.

## 📦 What's in the kit

- `word/` — the documents as `.docx` (Word, OneDrive, or upload to Google Drive)
- `markdown/` — the same documents as text (Google Docs "Paste from Markdown", Notion, Confluence)
- `sheets/` — the Ladder Assessment spreadsheet (Excel or Google Sheets; fill only the yellow cells)

## 🪜 Seven steps

1. Create a folder `Career Hub — <Person name>` and share it only with the person.
2. Upload or paste the documents. Self mode: 00–05. Lead mode: 00–06, plus 07 (level at risk) and 08 (new hire) when needed.
3. Upload the spreadsheet and open it as a spreadsheet.
4. Find and replace every `{{placeholder}}`, then search `{{` to check nothing is left.
5. Paste each document's link into the Career Hub table, and point every "← Back to Career Hub" link at the Hub.
6. After the documents are generated, paste your company's career ladder into the spreadsheet's Ladder tab. Pillar names differ by company.
7. Book a recurring 1:1 every two weeks with the Hub link in the invite.

> 💡 **The Growth Plan and the Focus Plan are alternatives.** Keep both for now; choose one together in the first 1:1 or Career Check.

> 🗓️ **The rhythm:** every two weeks, open the Hub, walk the status table, run the 1:1, update one document, update the statuses.
