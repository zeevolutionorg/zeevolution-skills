# Credits

These skills share my own experience leading and growing technologists. They also stand on public ideas I've found useful:

- **Brag documents** — Julia Evans, "Get your work recognized: write a brag document" (jvns.ca).
- **SBI feedback (Situation–Behavior–Impact)** — Center for Creative Leadership (ccl.org).
- **The Staff Engineer's Path** — Tanya Reilly (O'Reilly): maps, sponsorship and "boring" strategy.
- **Sponsorship ABCDs (Amplifying, Boosting, Connecting, Defending)** — Rosalind Chow.
- **The Manager's Path** — Camille Fournier (O'Reilly).
- **An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management** — Will Larson (lethain.com).
- **Repository shape** — inspired by Matt Pocock's skills repository on GitHub (mattpocock/skills).

All templates, the generic ladder and every example are original. Any resemblance of the fictional people or companies in the examples to real ones is coincidental.
